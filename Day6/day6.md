# Day 6 - Resume Optimization with Claude

## Objective

Learn how AI can analyze and optimize resumes for ATS (Applicant Tracking Systems).

## What I Did

* Uploaded my resume to Claude
* Used the Resume Optimizer Prompt
* Reviewed ATS analysis and feedback
* Generated an optimized one-page resume
* Compared the original and optimized versions

## Key Learnings

* ATS-friendly formatting improves resume readability
* Strong action verbs increase impact
* Relevant keywords help match job descriptions
* Quantifiable achievements make resumes stronger
* AI can quickly identify resume weaknesses

## Observations

The optimized resume was more structured, concise, and ATS-friendly compared to the original version. Claude suggested improvements in wording, formatting, and keyword usage.

Created a stronger and more professional resume using AI-assisted optimization techniques.
