Reference Basis Where the numbers in this tool come from
Energy (BMR/TDEE) Mifflin-St Jeor equation, scaled by activity multiplier (1.2–1.9×)
Protein target 1.6 g/kg body weight — general active-adult reference range
Iron, Vitamin C, Zinc, Magnesium Approximate adult RDA, gender-adjusted (ICMR-NIN / US-EU RDA tables)
Calcium, Vitamin D, B12, Folate, Vitamin A, Potassium, Fiber Standard adult daily reference values (general population, non-clinical)
Food composition data Approximate values per 100g, modeled on Indian food composition references (IFCT-style) and USDA FoodData Central for non-Indian items
Risk analysis thresholds Heuristic cut-offs (e.g. <60–70% of target) for educational flagging — not clinical diagnostic criteria

All values are population averages and approximations meant for general guidance. Actual nutrient content varies by brand, cooking method, and portion. This tool does not access or store any external database — all figures are built into the page.

NutriScope runs entirely in your browser — no data leaves this device.
