Business at a glance

Founded in 1981 (Bengaluru), Infosys is India's second-largest IT services company, serving 1,869+ active clients across 59+ countries. It provides consulting, technology outsourcing, cloud migration, AI-led transformation, cybersecurity, and next-gen digital services. Key proprietary platforms include Topaz (AI-first services), Cobalt (cloud), Finacle (banking), and McCamish (insurance). Revenue in USD terms crossed the $20 billion milestone in FY26.

Ownership context

Promoter holding is structurally low (~14.4%) as founders (Narayana Murthy family, Nilekani family) have diversified over time. This is a known and long-standing characteristic of Infosys, not a recent red flag.
High FII ownership (~30%+) signals sustained global institutional confidence. Major FIIs include Vanguard, Government Pension Investment Fund (Japan), and other index funds.
No promoter pledging — zero risk of forced selling due to pledge triggers.
Promoter reclassifications are ongoing (June 2026: Shreyas & Bhairavi Shibulal reclassified to public). This gradually reduces the already-low promoter percentage.

Overall fundamental quality
Strong
Infosys is a fundamentally sound, capital-efficient business with excellent return ratios, a debt-free balance sheet, and strong free cash flow generation. The near-term challenge is revenue growth acceleration in a soft IT demand environment, but the company's AI positioning (Topaz Fabric), record deal wins ($14.9B TCV FY26), and consistent margin delivery make it a high-quality franchise.

Key strengths

ROCE/ROE excellence: 40%+ ROCE and 32%+ ROE with zero financial leverage — genuinely asset-light, capital-efficient model.
FCF engine: $3.73B FCF in FY26 (112% conversion) — cash generation is reliable and supports a 3.56% dividend yield.
AI strategy (Topaz): Record large deal wins of $14.9B TCV with 55% net new — AI-led transformation deals are ramping.
Margin resilience: Maintained 20–21% adjusted operating margin despite wage hikes, fresher hiring, and macro headwinds for 3+ years.
Valuation discount: Trading at ~17x P/E vs 22–25x sector average and its own 5Y avg — potential mean-reversion opportunity if demand recovers.

Key watch points

Revenue growth: FY27 guidance of 1.5–3.5% CC is modest. US tariff uncertainty and soft discretionary IT spending may constrain recovery.
H-1B & immigration risk: High US revenue concentration (~60%) makes Infosys vulnerable to US visa policy changes and proposed fee hikes.
AI disruption risk: Generative AI could automate billable work, compressing headcount-linked revenues. Monitoring AI revenue contribution vs displacement is critical.
Low promoter holding: At ~14.4%, promoter skin-in-the-game is structurally low, though no pledge risk exists. Ongoing reclassifications reducing it further.
