# Day 14 – Job Red Flag Detector

## Objective

Analyze a job opportunity and company information using Claude AI to identify potential risks and positive indicators.

## Process

1. Gathered the Job Description and company information
2. Used the Job Red Flag Detector prompt
3. Generated a comprehensive risk analysis report
4. Reviewed the overall risk score, red flags, and positive signals
5. Evaluated the final verdict and interview preparation questions

## Report Highlights

* Overall Risk Score
* Positive Signals
* Red Flags
* Risk Breakdown
* Final Verdict
* Suggested Interview Questions

## Files Included

* Risk analysis report
* Job description
* Screenshots

## Key Learnings

* Evaluating job opportunities critically
* Identifying potential hiring risks
* Assessing company credibility
* Preparing relevant interview questions
