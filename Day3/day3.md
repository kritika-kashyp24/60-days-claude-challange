# Day 3 - Role-Based Prompting

## What I Worked On

Today, I explored Role-Based Prompting and learned how assigning a specific role changes the quality and perspective of AI responses.

## Question Used

How can I improve my resume?

## Prompt 1: Without Role

How can I improve my resume?

### Output
General suggestions about formatting, proofreading, and action verbs.

---

## Prompt 2: Founder Persona

Act as a startup founder and review my resume. How can I improve it?

### Output
Focused on impact, achievements, leadership, and business value.

---

## Prompt 3: Developer Persona

Act as a senior software developer and review my resume. How can I improve it?

### Output
Focused on technical skills, projects, GitHub profile, and coding experience.

---

## Key Learnings

- AI responses change based on assigned roles.
- Different personas provide different perspectives.
- Role-Based Prompting improves relevance and depth.
- Better prompts produce more useful outputs.

Role-Based Prompting is a powerful technique that helps generate expert-level responses tailored to specific needs.
